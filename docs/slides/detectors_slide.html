How MediCheck checks your bill

🔁
Duplicate Charges
High severity
The same procedure billed more than once on the same date of service. You should only ever pay for a service once.
How: compares CPT codes and service dates across all line items
📈
Medicare Rate Outlier
Medium severity
A billed amount unusually high compared to the official Medicare rate for that procedure. An outlier means the charge falls well outside the normal range — a signal it may be inflated.
How: compares each line item against locality-adjusted CMS Physician Fee Schedule rates
📋
EOB Mismatch
Medium severity
A discrepancy between what the provider billed you and what your insurer recorded on your Explanation of Benefits. When these don't match, someone made an error.
How: matches billed line items against EOB amounts by CPT code and date
⚖️
No Surprises Act Violation
High severity
An out-of-network charge at a facility where you expected in-network rates. Federal law protects you from these unexpected bills in most emergency situations.
How: flags out-of-network line items from EOB at in-network facility visits
Out-of-network
In-network means your insurer has a pre-negotiated rate with the provider — you pay a predictable, contracted amount. Out-of-network means no contract exists, so the provider can charge their full rate, which is often significantly higher.
